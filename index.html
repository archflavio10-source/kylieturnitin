What is a Turnitin similarity check?

A Turnitin similarity check compares your document against a large database of academic papers, journals, websites, and previously submitted student work. The result is a similarity report: a percentage showing how much of your text matches other sources, along with the specific passages involved.

Many universities and journals use Turnitin or comparable similarity-checking software as part of their submission process. SubmitReady is an independent service that gives you a similarity check of your own, before that official check happens.

Independent service: SubmitReady is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official partner of Turnitin, LLC. We provide independent similarity checking so you can review your document before you submit it.

Why students check similarity before submitting

Once your document reaches your university's official system, there's usually no chance to make changes. Checking beforehand gives you a preview of what that process might show, while you still have time to act on it.

  • Confirm that your citations and quotations look the way you intended
  • Spot passages that may need to be reworded or better attributed
  • Reduce last-minute uncertainty before a submission deadline
  • Walk into your defense, review, or journal submission better prepared

Common questions

Is this the same as submitting through my university's Turnitin account?+

No. This is an independent check you can run on your own, separate from whatever system your university uses. It's meant to help you review your document beforehand, not to replace your institution's own process.

Will the percentage match what my university sees exactly?+

Results can vary between similarity-checking tools and databases, so treat your report as a helpful reference rather than a guaranteed match to any specific system.

Can I check the same document more than once?+
